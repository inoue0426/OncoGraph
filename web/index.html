What can I use this for?

1. Start with a cancer type

Find a disease, then inspect recorded drug indications, CIViC profiles, genes, and two-step drug–gene–disease paths separately.

Example question: Which different kinds of evidence are recorded for a lung cancer subtype?

Search diseases →

2. Follow a drug's connections

Open a drug and trace each recorded link to a disease, including the target gene and original source when available.

Example question: How is osimertinib linked to diseases in this snapshot?

Open drug examples →

3. Review molecular evidence

Compare accepted CIViC evidence by gene, molecular profile, and cancer type; inspect the literature record and export an Evidence Tree.

Example question: What evidence is recorded for an EGFR T790M profile in a selected cancer type?

Compare profiles →

These are research questions about recorded sources. An approved-stage record is not a US FDA label; a trial is not evidence of benefit; a path through a gene is not an indication.
